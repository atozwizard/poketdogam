This page acts as a comprehensive list of all the New Mega Evolutions in the base game of Pokémon Legends: Z-A, so you can plan your Pokedex accordingly! If you want to see all of the new DLC Mega Evolutions in Mega Dimension, don’t miss out on that as well.
How Mega Evolution Works All New Mega Evolutions All Unrevealed Mega Evolutions How Mega Evolution Works Mega Evolution is temporary, only lasting as long as one battle, and can only be triggered if the trainer holds a Key Stone while the Pokemon holds its special Mega Stone . Mega Stones are first introduced during Mission 9: Chase That Mysterious Pokemon! In that mission, you will receive the Key Stone that is vital to harnessing the power of Mega Evolution.
But the Key Stone is only half of the puzzle. Each Pokemon requires their own unique Mega Stone to Mega Evolve. Many of these can be purchased, but you can also earn some throughout your adventure.
Check out our Mega Stone guide to track down any Mega Stone you need! In addition, Mega Evolution transformations are only temporarily usable in battles, instead of lasting the entire battle like in previous games. There is a Mega Evolution gauge that will go down over time as the Pokemon is in its super-powered form. Keep running around the arena and picking up the Mega Orbs that the enemy Pokemon releases to stay powered up for as long as possible!
