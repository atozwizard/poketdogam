Image: Nintendo Life With a brand new region comes a brand-new Pokédex. Pokémon Scarlet & Violet introduced multiple new Pokémon to discover, and in the Paldea region, you'll also find a ton of returning Pokémon and favourites to add to your team.
There are also some brand-new evolutions, and new forms, for some much-loved Pokémon. Some underappreciated 'mons have also finally been given a little bit of love.
