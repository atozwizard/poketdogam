Pokémon Gen 10 leaks just keep on coming, and as we approach Pokémon Day 2026 , new leaks and rumors will likely continue appearing online. Recent leaks have given a look at Gen 10's Pokédex, stating how many new Pokémon we can expect to find in the games. And if these leaks turn out to be true, then Gen 10 will be pretty ambitious. Over on Reddit , a user shared the leaks, with many Pokémon fans weighing in on how legitimate they thought the leaks were. Among the leaks were claims over how many new Pokémon were coming to the games, and speculation over what the final number of the Pokédex would hit.
How Many New Pokémon Will Be In Gen 10?
A Trainer rides their flying Koraidon in Pokémon Scarlet and Violet.
The leak claims there will be "about 100" new Pokémon in the Gen 10 games, including 8 new Legendaries. A few fans have speculated that the count is actually 150, a callback to the Gen 1 games and a way to celebrate the franchise's 30th Anniversary , which is occurring this year. Additionally, the latest rumor claims that these newbies will put the Pokédex for Gen 10 at around 400 Pokémon in total. That would put it on par with recent Pokémon games at launch. Sun and Moon had around 400 when all was said and done, and Sword and Shield had 400 at launch before getting any kind of DLC. Likewise, Scarlet and Violet had 400 before any DLC came out.
Pokémon Gen 10 Sounds Staggeringly Ambitious
Ash from Pokémon looking shocked with a Nintendo Switch. Custom Image by Katarina Cimbaljevic.
In terms of new Pokémon, though, the leaked figure of "about 100" would put it at about average. Earlier generations all had 100-150 new Pokémon added to them before Gen 6 only included around 72. The less-is-more approach continued until Paldea, when the Gen 9 games saw over 100 new Pokémon between the base game and DLCs.
Still, Gen 10 sounds really ambitious, from everything to its alleged map size and the way it's expected to include dozens of islands to explore, to an expansive Pokédex and new regional variants. Of course, these are all still leaks, so it's best to take everything with a grain of salt for now. Thankfully, we won't have long to wait to see how many of these leaks and rumors are true. Pokémon Gen 10 games are expected to debut as part of Pokémon Day 2026, which is February 27. That means there's a little over a week left until we hopefully get more info on the upcoming games and what to expect. Maybe we'll see some of these new Pokémon then too.
