IGN: Pokemon Winds and Waves region based on Southeast Asia February 28, 2026   Sickr 5 Comments
We got our first look at Pokemon Winds and Pokemon Waves yesterday during The Pokemon Presents presentation and it looks to be shaping up to be something special. The games are Nintendo Switch 2 exclusives, which mean that they should take advantage of the more powerful hardware and won’t be constrained by the original Nintendo Switch console. IGN has confirmed that the games take inspiration from the Philippines, Indonesia, and Malaysia with many users from those countries excitedly proclaiming that various landmarks found in the trailer largely resemble those seen in the Philippines and surrounding areas. Pokemon Winds and Pokemon Waves launch in 2027.
Thanks to SonicGalaxy27 for sending in the news tip!
