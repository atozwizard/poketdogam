In order to Gigantamax a Pokemon, you'll need to ensure it has the ability to do so, as only certain species in Pokemon Sword and Shield can Gigantamax. Not only does Gigantamax enhance a Pokemon's size like Dynamaxing , but it also changes its appearance entirely, too. This is a list of every Gigantamax Pokemon, complete with G-Max Move information and more.

Gigantamax Snorlax Gigantamax Snorlax G-Max Move: G-Max Replenish When Snorlax is Gigantamaxed, its Normal -type attacks will be transformed into G-Max Replenish. G-Max Replenish doesn’t merely deal damage to targets—it can also restore any Berries that Gigantamax Snorlax’s allies have eaten during the battle!
How to Get Gigantamax Snorlax Gigantamax Snorlax can be encountered in Max Raid Battles . During December 2019, Gigantamax Snorlax is more likely to spawn if you connect your Nintendo Switch to the internet.
Gigantamax Snorlax Gigantamax Charizard Gigantamax Charizard G-Max Move: G-Max Wildfire All Fire -type attacks used by a Gigantamax Charizard automatically change into G-Max Wildfire . This Fire -type attack doesn't do damage on a hit, but damages all Pokemon that aren't a Fire type over time for four turns.
How to Get Gigantamax Charizard See How to Get Gigantamax Charizard for more details because there's an easy, surefire way to get it after beating the game. You can get them through Max Raid Battles .
Gigantamax Charizard Gigantamax Pikachu Gigantamax Pikachu G-Max Move: Volt Crash All Electric -type moves used by a Gigantamax Pikachu automatically change into G-Max Volt Crash . This Electric -type attack damages and is guaranteed to Paralyze all opponents . This move will even Paralyze Ground -type Pokemon! How to Get Gigantamax Pikachu Gigantamax Pikachu can only be obtained as an Event Pokemon , specifically by having Pokemon Let's Go Pikachu save data on your Nintendo Switch. See How to Get Gigantamax Pikachu and Gigantamax Eevee for more details.
Gigantamax Pikachu Gigantamax Eevee Gigantamax Eevee G-Max Move: G-Max Cuddle All Normal -type attacks used by a Gigantamax Eevee automatically change into G-Max Cuddle . This Normal -type attack damages the opponent and infatuates Pokemon of the opposite gender.
How to Get Gigantamax Eevee Gigantamax Eevee can only be obtained as an Event Pokemon , specifically by having Pokemon Let's Go Eevee save data on your Nintendo Switch. See How to Get Gigantamax Pikachu and Gigantamax Eevee for more details.
Gigantamax Eevee Gigantamax Butterfree Gigantamax Butterfree G-Max Move: G-Max Befuddle All Bug-type attacks used by a Gigantamax Butterfree automatically change into G-Max Befuddle . This Bug-type attack damages the opponents and causes them all to become paralyzed, poisoned, or asleep.
How to Get Gigantamax Butterfree You can find Gigantamax Butterfree through Max Raid Battles .
Gigantamax Butterfree Gigantamax Meowth Gigantamax Meowth G-Max Move: G-Max Gold Rush All Normal -type attacks used by a Gigantamax Meowth automatically change into ' G-Max Gold Rush . How to Get Gigantamax Meowth Gigantamax Meowth can only be obtained as an Event Pokemon before January 15, 2020.

Gigantamax Rillaboom Gigantamax Urshifu Gigantamax Urshifu's appearance changes based on whether you have a Single Strike Style ( Fighting /Dark) or Rapid Strike Style ( Fighting /Water) Urshifu.
Gigantamax Urshifu G-Max Moves: G-Max One Blow and G-Max Rapid Flow Gigantamax Urshifu Single Strike Style has G-Max One Blow and Rapid Strike Style has G-Max Rapid Flow. One blow changes dark-type attacks into one blow. Rapid flow changes water-type attacks into rapid flow. These both ignore Protect and Max Guard.
How to Get Gigantamax Urshifu After completing unknown requirements in The Isle of Armor expansion , you'll be able to evolve the legendary Pokemon Kubfu into Urshifu. It can then Gigantamax. The form it takes while Gigantamaxed depends on the form you chose for Ushifu.
